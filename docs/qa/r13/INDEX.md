# r13 screenshots

Testimonial.to wall on the cream `p-speaker` section. Each shot is the viewport after scrolling to "What people say".

Consented shots have `nh-consent` set to accepted, so the iframe is loaded. The other shots leave the choice unset, so the cream placeholder stays and the embed is not requested.

The wall box is 800px tall at 1440 and 640px at 390, before and after the iframe mounts. Page CLS on these loads stayed under 0.04. On `/session`, where little else shifts, it stayed under 0.001.
